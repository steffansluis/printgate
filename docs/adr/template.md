Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN

## Context

The forces at play, stated as facts. What makes this a decision rather than an obvious step.

## Options

Each option seriously considered, with what it would cost. At least two; "keep things as they
are" usually counts as one.

## Decision

What we will do, in one or two sentences, active voice.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what is now out of bounds. Name any part of the
code that does not yet conform.
